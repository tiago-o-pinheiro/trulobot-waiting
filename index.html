The idea

Cute outside. Clear inside.

Trulobot is a character people can remember, with money rules people can inspect. These are proposed rules while the system is being tested.

01 / ONE TOKEN

One eventual token

We’re building toward one Base launch. There is no early-buy token or presale to join while we prepare it.

02 / OPEN LEDGER

Every received fee has a job

The proposed split of creator fees actually received is 55% holder rewards, 10% creator income, and 35% operations. Platform charges come first.

03 / WEEKLY CHECK

A check you can inspect

The planned Sunday check uses a full 24-hour balance window. Low funds, missing history, or an outage can mean rollover or delay, not a promised payout.
